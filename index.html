Thesis

Generative AI collapses weeks of early-stage production into hours. That's not a replacement — it's a shift in where your time actually goes. Mine went into taste, cultural judgment, and knowing when to push the output further and when to scrap it entirely. That's the thread through everything below — storyboards, brand campaigns, stage visuals — and six months of proving it on live briefs at Jio Creative Labs.

Case Study — Jio Creative Labs

From generating shots to directing an event.

Ownership grew month over month — from supporting an existing production to independently leading the AI-assisted creative direction of a large-scale live concert.

MONTH01

Learning Midjourney the hard way

Handed a script and a target of 64 storyboard shots for a confidential feature film — 25 delivered in the first month. Ran a full-cycle IPL commercial concept, an in-house short film, and design support on a government-facing project. The real lesson: Midjourney's lighting is inconsistent run to run, and that inconsistency has to be designed around, not fought.

MONTH02

First pitch, first campaign, first rewrite

Finished all 64 feature-film shots and cut a draft that was pitched to three production studios — two shortlisted it for further conversation. Pivoted the script after their notes on Act 2. Independence (FMCG) came in as a first independent campaign; generated and edited the Amagal Geet pitch video alongside it.

MONTH03

Onto the set

End-to-end involvement on a music video — planning, on-set, post. Built brand positioning and presentation for Celebrity Work, produced content for Shankar Ehsaan Loy, shipped an Independence Day ad, and shot the Milton campaign in-house. First tests of AI background generation and VFX pipelines began here.

MONTH04

Closing the VFX loop, opening Anandam

Completed and documented the green-screen and background-generation testing for future use across the team. Built the full pitch for Aeroplane Rice — logo, packaging, palette, go-to-market. Toward month's end, first concepts for Anandam's stage visuals began, anchored in the concert's devotional tone.

MONTH05

Anandam, full-scale

Turned early stage-design concepts into finished visuals: posters, digital promotional art, theme mockups — all AI-generated, all revised in close loop with the event's creative and production leads until the work felt like it belonged to Anandam, not just attached to it.

MONTH06

Anandam, live

The creative direction was finalized and deployed. This marked the culmination of the internship trajectory — starting from smaller support tasks and scaling up to fully managing the visual output for a major live event.

Flagship Project

EXECUTED — LIVE EVENT

A large-scale Bhakti devotional concert, co-produced by Jio Creative Labs. For the final two months of the internship, Swayam independently led the AI-assisted visual and stage direction — the single largest creative deliverable of the internship, and the one that pulled together everything learned before it.
